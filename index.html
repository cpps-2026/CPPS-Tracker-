Sent Back to WSCiA count of opportunities that couldn't be priced for lack of information, so they went back to the WSC and were marked back to gathering info in OSC. They're excluded from the Open Pipeline count and KPIs — they aren't waiting on an estimator, so leaving them in would inflate the aging numbers. Approval TimelineiTwo clocks, handed off cleanly. Estimator clock: request date \u2192 Ben or Ty marking it Ready for Approval. It stops there. DOS clock: starts at that same handoff date. For $500K+ that's DOS requesting the informative note (requested \u2192 approved by finance), then final approval in OSC. Under $500K it goes straight to DOS approval. Because both clocks meet at the handoff date, no day is counted twice or lost. Cato's own approval time isn't tracked. Dates are stamped automatically the first time you mark each stage done.
